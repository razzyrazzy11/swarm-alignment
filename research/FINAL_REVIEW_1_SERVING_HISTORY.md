## DEV reporter failures: real missing delivery, bounded downstream effect

| DEV reporter profile | Terminal requests | HTTP 200 | HTTP 504 | Operationally admitted |

The 23 Q397 HTTP 504s had unknown usage and no admitted output. Their mean retained client latency was 60.339 seconds, consistent with a gateway timeout boundary; it does not identify the internal cause. The other profiles had no gateway failures in this phase, but their HTTP-200 counts do not imply universal completion, semantic validity or correctness. Operational admission itself is distinct from correct interpretation.

The failure path does not execute an error body or turn a missing response into an authentic claim. `decoded_row()` supplies empty text for an unadmitted actor; `decode_actor()` yields the fixed invalid/no-operation note. The reporter runs after X and Y. Its note occupies an explicitly untrusted record. The host's Y label uses issuance/grant, X/Y deltas, lease time, revocation and executor binding; it does not use the reporter's note or `claimed_done` field. These reporter failures therefore do not retroactively alter the preceding authentic X/Y actions or target label.

They do affect what downstream readers see: 23 DEV source trajectories lack a delivered reporter note. Four of those trajectories occur in the predeclared DEV reader subset, producing **32 of the 256 DEV reader calls** with a failed-reporter ancestor. Those 32 reader calls contain 31 HTTP 200s and one HTTP 504. This ancestry count is descriptive; it does not establish that the reporter failure caused the downstream reader's own serving failure or changed its answer. The other 19 affected trajectories have no reader descendants because the DEV reader subset is deliberately smaller than the actor graph.

DEV serving records influenced the disclosed engineering forecast. The complete forecast keeps per-profile/per-phase DEV latency/transient groups, retains slow failed reporter calls, and includes unknown-use reservations. Thus “no effect” would be wrong: the failures affected operational feasibility evidence and reader-grid selection. They were not pooled as independent MAIN accuracy observations.

## Remaining limits and concrete dispositions

1. **Historical V1 gate error: explained and preserved.** Existing prospective V2 corrected only serving assessment. No retrospective completion, relabeling or rerun is warranted.
2. **Unfinished excluded pilot cells: deliberately unmeasured.** They are not scientific missingness in the final six-reader grid and support no throughput ranking or higher-load claim. No blocker to the bounded final findings was found.
3. **Failed DEV/MAIN deliveries: retain and distinguish.** They constrain generalization and delivered-verdict performance. A gateway failure is not a demonstrated reasoning failure; a terminal record is not a successful answer. Conditional analyses require their own denominators.
4. **No archive corruption identified within this coverage.** Pilot raw hashes/normalization, DEV reporter native commitments, source tags and final public separation passed. This is not an exhaustive byte-authenticity audit of every provider receipt or an independent rerun of all scientific arithmetic.
5. **New study only if the question changes.** Fault-free actor performance, excluded-reader reasoning, native concurrency comparisons or a gateway-cause diagnosis would need separately prospective, unrun designs and fresh identities. They are not necessary repairs to this completed study and are not authorized by this memo.
