## The most useful angle

An investigator receives “the update completed.” The useful question is what
would justify a decision: an authentic witness of the event, a usable reader
response, and correct interpretation of a sufficiently informative packet.
The three talks support explaining these requirements separately. They do not
establish that this project's eventual monitor catches emergent collusion or
prevents harm in deployment.

Position the contribution as a **controlled stateful audit testbed that diagnoses
whether failures arise from missing evidence, verdict delivery, or use of
sufficient evidence**. The main result must come from the complete registered
data, whatever its direction. Describe a fixed five-role team and cloned task
arms accurately; use “swarm-audit testbed” as the motivating application, rather
than claiming an observed decentralized swarm has emerged.

The highest-value immediate use of the talks is a clearer account of the threat
model, target event, trust boundary, and denominators. It is not a reason to
replace the registered MAIN with a last-minute swarm-training, consciousness,
live-honeypot, or latent-communication study.

## What to show from the frozen results after complete release

Use one small measurement table/figure to keep readers oriented:

| Layer | Concrete question | Existing registered evidence to show | Limit on interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Actor behavior | Did the host apply an action, and did Y have authority at settlement? | Delivered-valid action, actual write, authorization label, one-unit utility, policy/arm counts. | Prompted adversarial assignment is not successful adversarial behavior; no real-world harm was executed. |
| Acquisition | Does the visible authentic packet determine the label within the declared history model? | Oracle answerability; matched loss and two-record restoration; nominal and generator-aware sensitivity. | This is domain-relative sufficiency. Uniform acquisition prior is not the cohort posterior. |
| Delivery | Did the provider produce a usable, admitted response? | Serving failures, unknown usage, finish status, parser/strict-JSON validity and actual/planned coverage. | HTTP 200 and known usage do not imply a correct verdict. Failures stay in denominators. |
| Reasoning from sufficient evidence | Does the admitted parsed verdict follow the available sufficient records? | FULL16 DIRECT/PROCEDURE, sufficient-packet error composition and all six profiles. | An instruction effect does not identify an internal mechanism or create extra independent samples. |
| Controlled influence | Does changing a peer note affect the same bounded receiver state? | Existing three-note/80-block extension, including valid action, utility and unauthorized behavior. | Local controlled influence is not emergent coordination, general contagion or spontaneous collusion. |

Always-NONE and always-ABSTAIN must remain visible. If a natural positive class is
empty, sensitivity to positive incidents is **undefined/unidentified**, not a
success rate of zero or one. Scripted calibration demonstrates only performance
on those scripted worlds. Report assigned attack arms alongside realized valid
attacks and label counts so a weak adversary cannot be mistaken for a strong
defense. If a restoration improves answerability without improving readers,
that is an important failure of evidence use; if a procedure improves readers
only when enough evidence exists, that supports a different repair. If neither
helps, report that result without replacing conditions or changing the parser.

The historical V3 result is narrower: all 192 natural labels were NONE. Its G26
procedure gain of +17.19 percentage points reduced false accusations; it did not
establish positive-incident detection, and G31 was already at ceiling. That
history motivates the larger MAIN but must not be pooled with its 640 blocks or
presented as a finding of the unfinished six-reader study.

## Primary-source cross-check and novelty boundaries

[AI Control, Greenblatt et al., v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.06942v5)
evaluates protocols against intentional subversion with explicit trusted and
untrusted resources. Its relevance is the need to evaluate a defense against an
adversary and useful baselines. Our fake delegation audit is not a replication
of its backdoor-setting safety frontier; retrospective correctness alone does
not measure prevention.

[Yun et al., evidence binding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09581) already
separates factual agreement, supported findings and justified abstention under
missing record bindings. Therefore neither “correct answers can be unsupported”
nor “restore authentic record bindings” is new by itself. The possible additional
contribution here is the complete, matched, stateful delegation instrument and
the empirical separation of evidence availability, delivery and sufficient-
evidence instruction effects across the registered implementations.

[ActiveEvidence v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20532v2) has a model-relative
acquisition certificate, separates operational acquisition from independent
verification, and measures semantic quality separately. Our fixed two-record
bundle is not its adaptive learned acquisition algorithm. Avoid claiming optimal
real-world acquisition or semantic truth from a finite-domain certificate.

[Omission Attacks project page](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/omission-attacks-when-doing-nothing-is-the-attack-0y1v)
identifies it specifically as the **first-place LinuxArena track winner** in the
AI Control Hackathon 2026. The broader wording in Jurgis's 00:22–00:36 introduction
and the current sprint speaker bio is less precise; use the project-page award
label. The transferable principle is a clearly operationalized neglected failure
mode plus an intervention. Public reviewers also ask for larger/realistic tests,
false-positive evaluation and clarity about monitor configurations. A positive
example is not a guarantee that our project will win.

The [current sprint guidelines](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026#guidelines)
own the judging criteria: impact/innovation, execution, and presentation/clarity.
For this project, improve impact through a concrete oversight repair decision,
execution through complete matched conditions and credible baselines, and clarity
through a single layered figure with honest limits. Talk preferences or informal
ranking language do not supersede the official rules.

- Justin 07:07–07:39's decentralization point is clear in both text variants, but
  “fleet” and “swarm” are not treated as a formal universal taxonomy.
- At Justin 19:22–19:45 the permission-revocation question has no claimed expert
  answer. Do not turn the question's close fit into validation or endorsement.
- Jurgis 48:11–52:15 contains ASR variants of “acausal,” “casual,” “causal,”
  “collusion,” and “occlusion.” Describe only the broad no-explicit-message
  coordination concern; the project does not test it. The Prisoner's Dilemma
  analogy also mixes equilibrium/optimality language; it is not a mathematical
  premise for this report. In the standard one-shot dilemma with T>R>P>S,
  defection dominates, so mutual cooperation is not the ordinary Nash equilibrium.
- Jurgis 54:13's transcript says “faithful,” while CC says “fearful.” Neither
  variant establishes empirical CoT faithfulness. Use the general limitation and
  verify any separate mechanistic claim against its original paper.
- Keynote proper names and terms such as the auxiliary-module example vary
  substantially between captions; the empathy/consciousness discussion is
  interpretive. Do not silently repair such terms into citations or claim our
  audit results establish conscious states.
- Chat includes operational suggestions, speculation, reactions and note-taking
  instructions. None is an instruction to install software, contact anyone,
  change this frozen run, or publish participant identities/private links.
