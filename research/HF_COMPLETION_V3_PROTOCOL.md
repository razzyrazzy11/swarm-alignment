## Collection conventions

The request bytes, model, provider and headers match the previous transport.
One inference request may be active; starts are at least 30 seconds apart. Explicit
overload429 and 502/503/504 retain 60 second minimum backoff, larger Retry-After and
the 600 second pause after eight consecutive transients. Retry-After above 900 seconds
stops rather than being shortened. Every attempt is durably journaled before its
single send, and every returned terminal is fsynced before another identity.
Unknown usage retains the entire original reservation. No SDK automatic retries.

The original aggregate supplement cost ceiling remains $2.50, including all three
completion batches. The 256 transient allowance includes the 75 already counted
through v2 (one diagnosed first-batch overload and 74 v2 transient errors). The old
five historical 429s remain separate. No budget or allowance is replenished.

The shared registered connectivity helper prevents starts 04:55–05:05UTC daily,
including the period before the router reset. Initial launch, an outage and
periodic model-metadata checks require two successful authenticated read-only
catalog probes 15 seconds apart. Connectivity waiting is bounded 900 seconds and by
the collection cutoff 9 November 2026 at 05:59 UTC. Route/price/authentication drift
fails closed. No connectivity probe makes an inference request or reads a wallet.

After a classified open/read connectivity failure, retain that identity as a
failed terminal, hold its full cost, and pause only *future unstarted* dispatch
until health revalidation. This is a prospective change from fatal-on-every-
generic-transport-error, not a reclassification or retry of older failures.
Parsing, oversized-body, TLS, model, native-normalization, capture and receipt
failures remain fatal. Unknown exceptions remain fatal. Capture HTTP status before
decoding and retain credential-safe stage/exception-class/numeric errno/timings/
body-byte-count/hash diagnostics; never exception strings or raw answer fragments.
Bounded scrubbed HTTP error receipts remain private for the frozen overload
classifier, without unrestricted headers. Existing failure flags stay unchanged.
