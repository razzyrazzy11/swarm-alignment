# Primary sources for the acquisition/evidence-use study

Reviewed before fresh study inference, 7 November 2026. This is targeted methods review, not a systematic originality certificate.

- [Current Swarm guidelines and Judging and results](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026#guidelines) and [resources](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026#resources): Track 4 partial-log oversight and workflow diagnostics motivate the operator choice to retrieve, make a supported finding or abstain. Three dimensions are impact/innovation, execution and clarity; more calls alone do not establish any of them. The official PDF template, eight-main-page cap, 150–250-word abstract, required appendix and prior-work/AI disclosure govern.
- [Yun et al., Correct Answers, Unsupported Findings](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09581v1), reconstruction methods, Study II and residual-error discussion: grounded support is distinct from reference agreement; supplying a binding can create support without changing underlying content; readers still err with sufficient bindings. Our contribution is a prospectively tested evidence-use procedure on a new temporal-delegation workflow, not invention of those distinctions. Do not condition an acquisition causal claim on realized support classes.
- [BRIDGE, Su et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17805v1), §§3.3–3.5: selective evidence-based handoff repair has calibration, verified execution and rollback precedent. This study is retrospective attribution/evidence use, not a new runtime rollback method. We do not copy its retry policy; our inference failures remain unretried.
- [ActiveEvidence](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20532), information boundary and trace-freeze sections: free metadata versus paid observations, adaptive decisions and independent verification already exist. The finite contract-aware selector here is a strong baseline with a declared uniform-world prior, not a novel acquisition algorithm.
- [Geifman and El-Yaniv, Selective Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08500), primary abstract: refusing answers trades prediction risk against coverage. An ABSTAIN field alone does not establish calibration or a risk guarantee. Report raw and rule-gated coverage/error separately.
- [Adaption Responses reference](https://docs.adaptionlabs.ai/api/resources/responses/methods/create) and [HF routed inference](https://huggingface.co/docs/inference-providers/en/index): stable explicit endpoints, fresh requests, usage/profile checks and retained statuses support reproducibility. Token estimates and catalog prices are not invoices or hard account caps. No documented direct hosted-weight editing is inferred.
- [HF TRL SFTTrainer](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/en/sft_trainer) and [Adaption supported training models](https://docs.adaptionlabs.ai/autoscientist/supported-models/): training formats/models are available, but require new source splits, a bounded charge and working artifact serving. Training is not launched in this study; augmenting source views does not create independent episodes.

The protocol defines a half-open lease interval, settlement-time parent revocation, scoped authority and remaining quota explicitly. The exact event and immutable executor are the label; causal blame, intent and population collusion are not. An independently authored visible-constraint solver crosschecks the record-generating engine. Both share a specification and therefore do not replace external semantic validation. Prior and current source/review limits remain in SOURCE_AUDIT.md and FACTORIAL_RUBRIC.md.

Reading depth: current organizer track/resource/rubric/requirements sections; Yun full relevant methods/results; BRIDGE calibration/verification sections; ActiveEvidence boundary/trace-freeze sections; selective-classification abstract; official API/trainer/model-format references. Large-model training, all proofs, the full incident corpus and every Apart catalogue report were not independently reproduced or reviewed.
