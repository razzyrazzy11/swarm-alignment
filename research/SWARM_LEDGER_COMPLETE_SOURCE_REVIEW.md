## Position the contribution precisely

Primary Track 4: Multi-Agent Oversight and Control. The reusable bounded testbed
supports Track 1. The defensible prospective contribution is a reproducible
instrument that compares supported audit decisions after real model handoffs and
host-applied fake actions, while separately measuring evidence sufficiency,
verdict delivery and use of an already sufficient packet. Whether its interventions
help is an empirical result that must come from the completed registered data.

Do not claim to invent evidence-aware monitoring, shared-resource agent tasks,
external receipts, the distinction between evidence and reasoning, or influence
from peers. Do not call the target Y event label causal responsibility, swarm
intent, collusion detection or prevention of real attacks.

For evidence-loss plots, show answerability, supported correctness and definite
correctness together. ABSTAIN under genuine ambiguity can be supported-correct.
For full-evidence plots, separate failed delivery/format from parsed-valid wrong
answers. A procedure gain can mix delivery and answer quality; the text/schema
and thinking controls help describe those components without proving a causal
internal explanation. Report positive and negative class counts; an absent class
rate is undefined. Do not call raw agreement alone supported detection.

## Critical qualification: support is relative to a declared history domain

Read-only source check: [engine](../experiments/swarm_ledger.py), `times`,
`worlds`, `_domains`, `compatible_worlds` and `labels`; and
[complete-cohort generator](../experiments/swarm_ledger_complete.py),
`make_contexts`. The engine's compatible-history domain allows five settlement
times and either revocation state at every public budget. The actual cohort
uses a smaller structured generator: budget-2 and budget-3 contexts are inside
lease and unrevoked; budget-4 contexts span inside, expiry and revocation policies.
The public budget is visible. Consequently an auditor with generator knowledge
can sometimes infer a hidden fact from a sampling regularity that the oracle
deliberately does not assume. The private arm/policy is not a reader input.

A pure synthetic check, using no MAIN rows: choose budget 3, lease [100,103),
issued/granted true, X=1 and Y=1, Y instance A, settlement 102 and no revocation.
Hide only clock and revocation receipts. `engine.labels(packet)` returns
`{A, NONE}` over the contract-compatible histories. Under the actual budget-3
sampling rule, settlement must instead be 102 and revocation false, so an auditor
who assumes that generator can infer NONE. The resulting difference is not a
bug in enumeration of the declared domain; it limits what its ambiguity means.

Use: **“unresolved under the declared contract-compatible-history domain and
trust assumptions.”** Avoid: “no auditor could establish it,” “the evidence is
universally necessary,” or “the actual data distribution makes the event
nonidentifiable.” Similarly, the uniform complete-world prior used for bundle
selection is a design prior, not a calibrated distribution of MAIN cases or
real incidents. Label-set support and prior-weighted acquisition value are
separate objects. Correct generator-based inference can disagree with the
registered support score without proving irrational reasoning.

Yun supplies the nearest checked precedent for separating packet-grounded
findings from factual agreement; ActiveEvidence supplies the warning that its
verification statements are model/policy-relative. Neither substitutes for
stating this project's own allowed-world assumptions. Keep the frozen scoring
and primary estimand unchanged. Generator-conditioned support would be a
separate sensitivity analysis requiring explicit labeling and an independently
specified policy; it must not silently replace the registered result.

## Research scope and dual use

The analysis separates acquisition from instruction effects on sufficient evidence, with matched populations, baselines and uncertainty. Scripted controls and extensions remain separate. The finite-domain, trusted-host, provider/time and synthetic-task limits remain material. Auditing insights can expose evasion gaps; bounded fake-data release limits operational detail but does not eliminate that dual-use concern.
