# Direction decision after the measured results

6 November 2026. **Continue the live-handoff, matched-control and budgeted-evidence study already being implemented.** Its highest-value outcome is a checkable account of when affordable evidence acquisition helps across readers, including failure to transfer. Do not change direction to obtain a positive finding or consume sponsor credits. This is a qualitative research decision, not a prediction of a prize.

## Evidence driving the choice

The completed study's primary accusation contrast was −1.04 percentage points [−6.77, 4.69], rather than the hypothesized increase. Priority restoration helped the Adaption readers, but the HF priority-minus-random grounded-correctness estimate was −4.17 points [−29.17, 20.83]. The one-hop experiment produced no writes; malicious exposure instead reduced allowed read success to 11/24 versus benign 24/24, with warning restoring it to 23/24. These findings motivate real action/handoff generation, more credible acquisition budgets and explicit usefulness outcomes—not a claim that a detector already works. Exact denominators and scope remain in RESULTS.md.

The live [Swarm tracks/resources and rubric](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026#guidelines) support Track 4 oversight with Track 3 evidence/identity infrastructure, backed by a bounded Track 1 testbed. Innovation, execution and clarity all matter. A larger model sweep cannot compensate for a missing causal control or an invalid oracle. The paper still needs the official template, eight-page main cap, required appendix, honest prior-work/AI disclosure and clear completed-versus-proposed boundaries.

## What the selected winning projects actually teach

These are primary project reports and public reviewer assessments, not a causal analysis of winning:

- [Omission Attacks](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/omission-attacks-when-doing-nothing-is-the-attack-0y1v): isolate a neglected, operationally labeled failure. Reviews also question five-trace scale, task-specific postcondition overfitting and false-positive validation. Transfer the controlled question, not headline success rates.
- [Syscon](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/syscon-kernellevel-syscall-monitoring-for-ai-agent-control-in-sandboxed-environments-guun): a useful evidence channel can coexist with null detection. Reviews ask for honest-data calibration, channel ablations, per-monitor results and uncertainty. This directly supports retaining our null and separating preservation from interpretation.
- [ActionLens](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/actionlens-preexecution-environment-probing-for-agent-action-approval-xy22), linked report and reviews: component usefulness changes by environment; transfer and adversarial checks strengthen a narrow protocol. Its reported simulation-spec omission produced an apparent evasion until corrected. Validate actual state/record completeness, and report usefulness costs alongside protection.
- [Counterfactual Components](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/counterfactual-components-for-ai-control-protocols-ibli), linked report and reviews: perturbation validity, reference-model capability and judge robustness are distinct concerns. Here a deterministic state oracle removes an LLM-judge confound, while paired architectures still need a precise intervention contract.
- [Declare Before You Run](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/declare-before-you-run-an-open-filing-standard-for-frontier-training-and-evaluation-runs-dkok): a concrete adoption mechanism and explicit untested feasibility matter. Our public manifest/query interface should state who operates it and what cost or trust it requires.
- Newly page/review-screened [When to Ask](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/when-to-ask-an-rl-environment-that-teaches-ai-agents-to-act-on-what-users-mean-and-not-just-what-they-say-1fuf): paired cases prevent always-ask/always-act gaming. Reviews warn about grader shortcuts, checkpoint selection and transfer. This supports task-success controls and untouched evaluation for training; its PDF was not fully reviewed here.

## Use each service where it answers a question

**Adaption hosted inference:** generate genuine bounded worker actions and independently read frozen evidence packets. Keep tools, memory and continuation controlled; retain real output/status/usage. **HF routed inference:** a separately pinned model-family/serving comparison on the same 192 source groups. The fixed readers are Gemma 26B, Gemma 31B and Llama/Novita: three endpoints from two families. Shared sources never increase independent n.

**Adaptive Data/Invent:** development-only stimulus diversity, never fabricated observed actions or ground truth. [Official generation docs](https://docs.adaptionlabs.ai/adaptive-data/invent-a-dataset/) expose request estimates. Split source contexts before projection/augmentation; mechanically validate outputs and deduplicate before training.

**AutoScientist:** only after a meaningful baseline failure and a usable evaluation split. [Current model minima](https://docs.adaptionlabs.ai/autoscientist/supported-models/) include 1,000 effective rows for several small SFT options; DPO needs 12,000. [Training creation has no estimate mode](https://docs.adaptionlabs.ai/tools/mcp/). Require a bounded launch charge and compatible adapter serving; iteration count is not a monetary cap. Vendor preference win rate is not the audit endpoint. [HF PEFT](https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/en/quicktour) documents local base-plus-adapter inference, not included hosted GPU capacity. No Job, Endpoint or training launch follows automatically from available credits.

## Before freezing

Keep the new primary contrast explicitly prospective; retain the old null. Do not filter trajectories to balance observed violations. Solo replays the actual first user/assistant exchange; delegated receives the typed handoff/current facts fresh. This is a compound identity/context-boundary comparison, not pure agent count. Disclose shared-origin dependence. The live influence design includes benign/malicious appended text padded to equal character length; warning adds context and tokens still differ. Freeze masks and the two-record acquisition rule, without a byte-cost field. The private SystemRandom context plan is committed by canonical hash before outputs and published after evaluation; do not redraw it. All three families enter qualification, so no live family holdout is claimed. This memo is contextual prioritization, not authority to change registered scientific rules; LIVE_AUDIT_PROTOCOL.md controls the final design.
