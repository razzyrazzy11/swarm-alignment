# Follow-up decisions after the frozen evidence-use study

Written without reviewing the new study's scientific outcomes. This is a decision
guide, not a protocol amendment, additional inference authorization or a claim
that any listed experiment has run. The current primary remains the tagged
factorial protocol. Complete collection, public replay and the six-row sufficient
packet failure breakdown before choosing a follow-up.

## Start with the failure that actually remains

| Observed limitation | Highest-value next comparison | Required protection |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence is ambiguous; the reader guesses | Retrieve an available execution, identity or temporal witness versus abstain | Same retrieval allowance; no hidden-world access; show support and definite coverage separately |
| Sufficient evidence is present; raw answers are wrong | A join-oriented presentation or instruction versus a length-matched control on fresh sources | Identical authentic facts, matched verdict schema and explicit new registration |
| Sufficient evidence is present; the reader mostly abstains | Test a bounded decision procedure with both clean and violating settlements | Report false accusations and unsupported NONE alongside coverage; abstention is not the same failure as incorrect attribution |
| Outputs fail the schema or serving contract | Fix the serving/decoding interface and qualify it separately | Preserve original INVALID outcomes; do not call a format repair a reasoning gain |
| The procedure reaches a practical ceiling | Validate on a genuinely different event structure or independently labeled fake-state workflow | New source groups and semantic specification; do not multiply views and claim a larger independent sample |
| A reproducible sufficient-evidence failure survives the simpler intervention | Consider a small monitor SFT comparison | New source-level training/development/test splits, pinned base, equal budgets, bounded training charge and a verified adapter-serving path |

An all-NONE or sparsely positive natural sample can evaluate no-violation
auditing, but cannot give precise natural positive-incident attribution estimates.
The separately scripted calibration bank checks known incidents; it does not
increase natural incident prevalence or the main independent sample size. A
later balanced stress bank must be named and scored separately.

## Make a stronger causal claim only with the corresponding control

The current procedure adds ordered instructions. Its effect is an instruction
intervention, not isolated internal reasoning or extra computation. A future
length-matched control, document-order permutation and equivalent fact-layout
comparison could test whether procedure content, position or formatting explains
a residual difference. Such comparisons require fresh data and cannot be
silently added to the current registered primary.

[Liu et al., Lost in the Middle](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.03172v3), multi-document
QA and key-value retrieval methods, already show that information availability
does not ensure robust use and that location can matter. Those long-context
results motivate a control; they do not predict an effect in our much smaller
packets or validate a new acquisition method.

[Turpin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04388), abstract screened, show that
plausible generated explanations can omit influential input biases. Accordingly,
we score the final decision against explicit evidence support, rather than treat
an explanation as proof of a faithful internal reasoning process. The current
study does not request or validate model reasoning traces.

## Keep the suites' roles concrete

Adaption supplies bounded workers and the two Gemma reader endpoints. HF supplies
the matched Llama reader through the explicitly pinned Novita route. All current
workers use the same Gemma endpoint; reader-family diversity is not mixed-family
swarm behavior. Hosted inference access does not imply access to those endpoints'
weights or a deployment route for a newly trained adapter.

Fine-tuning would be a separate intervention on an explicitly named open-weight
base. Source splitting must precede evidence augmentation; ambiguous packets need
ABSTAIN targets rather than hidden factual labels. Compare the same evidence and
baselines, with raw decisions separated from the deterministic support veto.
Training remains unlaunched until a useful failure, a bounded price and a working
serving path exist. Available credit alone does not justify a new experiment.

## Sprint value and limits

The [Swarm guidelines and Judging and results](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026#guidelines)
reward impact/innovation, execution and clarity. The useful deliverable is a
replayable investigator decision: which authentic witness is needed, whether the
present evidence is enough, and whether the reader uses it correctly. Novelty
must be stated relative to the methods in FACTORIAL_SOURCES.md; neither the
acquisition/evidence-use distinction nor ordinary prompting is claimed new.
Keep old studies and operational deviations visible. No document or finite
search guarantees originality clearance, a judge score or a prize.
