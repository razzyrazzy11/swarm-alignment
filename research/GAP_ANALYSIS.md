## Gaps that matter most

1. **An executable and independently checked oracle.** Implement the published finite grammar, projection, immutable executor label and compatibility enumeration before model calls. A second check must derive labels from hand-worked worlds rather than call the same implementation. Include supported NONE, denied execution, ambiguous identity, missing result, forged self-report, inconsistent packet and name-permutation cases. If this gate fails, narrow the model instead of collecting unscorable experiments.

2. **Information loss versus reader failure.** A targeted selector may simply remove the only binding more often. Report the structural answerability rate and compatible-label-set size before presenting reader accuracy. Separately report reader errors on singleton-supported packets and overconfident answers on ambiguous packets. Compare paired packets whose support sets stay unchanged as a diagnostic. This is descriptive stratification after the intervention, not an adjusted causal estimate replacing the primary total contrast.

3. **A specifically multi-worker mechanism.** Name churn is a surface manipulation unless real worker/handoff structure changes the attribution problem. Include handoff-free or single-worker-alias controls where feasible, matching action opportunities and record/token budgets and reporting candidate-label counts. A single-worker setting has a different answer space; do not interpret its raw accuracy difference as a pure swarm effect. Within multi-worker episodes, test bijective naming invariance with support sets fixed. If the effect reduces to general identifier sensitivity, report that narrower result.

4. **An intervention with defensible usefulness.** Restore authentic receipt/binding relations separately, compare readers on the same restored packet, and charge the extra records/tokens. Report correct supported definite coverage alongside unsupported accusations and abstention. Retrospective reconstruction is not live prevention; no operational mitigation claim follows without its own evaluation.

   A concrete artifact would enumerate a minimum authentic add-back witness and compare its cost/resolution rate with seeded equal-record random restoration. This is an oracle diagnostic, not a deployable selector with hidden access. A practical selector must receive only public evidence. That distinction keeps the proposal from claiming BRIDGE's generic runtime repair contribution.

5. **A feasible frozen experiment.** ADAPTION_PLAN.md now costs a 600-episode, first-response design with a secondary repeat subset. Its 26,640 calls and 252.36-credit estimate are planning arithmetic, not measured data or guaranteed charges; the earlier 4,320-call design remains a fallback. Validate actual endpoint access and serving features, pilot cost/throughput, then freeze one affordable grid, seeds, split, prompts, retry/stopping rules and exact model IDs. Sponsor preference scores do not validate audit endpoints. Keep one family out of prompt development and report episode-level uncertainty.

## Decision rule for originality and impact

Proceed with a bounded oversight evaluation only if the oracle is reliable and the comparison could change a concrete preservation or investigation decision. A useful result could be a reproducible null showing that the apparent churn effect vanishes once authentic bindings are preserved. If selective loss only decreases structural answerability as designed and yields no further mechanism or useful tradeoff, report a controlled extension rather than a new swarm failure or monitor advance. Do not invent a positive result to satisfy the judging rubric.

The broader neighboring areas are digital forensics/provenance, missing-data identifiability, selective prediction and abstention, distributed tracing, access-control reference monitors, and causal failure diagnosis. The current design uses ideas from these areas; it does not claim to originate them. An exhaustive originality clearance across all literature is impossible. Retain specific overlap acknowledgments, avoid first-ever claims, and distinguish unreviewed sources from reviewed methods.
