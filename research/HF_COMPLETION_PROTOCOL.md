## Collection controls

One active request, at least 30 seconds between actual HTTP opener starts. Each start is durably journaled before sending; a terminal is durable before the next identity. No started identity is retried, even on an HTTP error. Unresolved starts halt recovery. A resume uses only the exact never-started complement under the same registration.

Preserve bounded, credential-scrubbed error bodies privately (16 KiB), and publish no raw headers, native IDs or error bodies. Explicit service-overload HTTP 429 responses and HTTP 502/503/504 may move to the next identity after at least 60 seconds or the longer Retry-After. Every eight consecutive eligible transient failures triggers a 600-second circuit pause. There is a new maximum of 256 eligible transient failures; the original five 429s remain separately recorded and are not reset or erased. Unknown 429s, authentication/payment errors, model or token drift, failed capture, unresolved starts, STOP or budget exhaustion halt dispatch. These gates depend on transport/configuration, not task correctness, parsed verdict, class or score. Known truncations remain INVALID under the original scorer and are not selected out.

Original metadata and raw-prefix commitments are verified before collection. The exact HF route and rates are checked against a read-only catalog before launch and every 100 completed jobs. No neutral inference pilot is included: earlier serving/capacity probes remain in the operational record, and technical safety continues to be monitored during every new call. No long-run availability guarantee follows from a catalog entry.

## Resource and timing limits

The new stage has a $2.50 estimated-cost/reservation cap, separate from the original $0.880229785 HF audit estimate. Unknown usage retains the complete original worst-case per-request reservation of $0.0048784. Known usage uses frozen $0.135 input/$0.40 output per million tokens. These are estimates, not invoices or hard account caps. At previously observed average known usage, remaining calls would cost approximately $0.82; this is a planning estimate, not a promise. All 1,898 worst-case reservations would exceed the last reported $5.29 account balance, so the collector reserves one request at a time and stops before the next reservation exceeds its stage cap. No top-up, subscription change or new provider account is authorized by this protocol.

At 30 seconds per start, 1,898 calls take at least 15.82 hours, with additional time for service pauses. The original plan is complete only when all original identities have terminal records; successful serving and valid verdicts must be reported separately. Do not call a plan complete solely because a source or budget check passed.
